Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-25
  Info:
    - First release. Made with AI: code, balance, texts and tests by Claude (Anthropic) in Claude Code,
      pictures by ChatGPT, directed and playtested by a human.
  Features:
    - Five epochs from stone to the Moon: clay tablets, glass flasks, mechanisms, reactive, sea charts and
      instrument boards as science. The vanilla tech tree, starting items and biters are gone.
    - Petrification waves: the first after 2 hours, then more and more often. One revival chamber per team
      makes a sealed charge (I–V) that wakes everyone at once. A green flash and stone on the screen edges
      while petrified. Defeat if a wave finds the team still stone; victory on the Moon.
    - A start without chores: a digger, a bow and stone arrows with no research, faster hands, cheap clay
      tablets, a woodlot that grows wood, a "Next step" hint and a diary.
    - Wild animals in packs with leaders: territory, a leash, short night raids, and they leave a dead
      player's body. Respawn with a bow and arrows.
    - Weapons from the bow to sulfur gunpowder, turrets, sharper arrowheads I–III.
    - Climate belts, continents over a deep ocean, landfill and deep landfill, ships on waterways.
    - Ruins with notes: hints, alternative recipes, caches.
    - Factoriopedia shows only what exists in the game; every technology says what its research takes.
  Graphics:
    - Own icons, ships, campfire with animated flames, scholar's desk and lairs; the rest is recoloured
      vanilla for now.
