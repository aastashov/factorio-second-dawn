Version: 0.10.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Major Features:
    - Epoch 5 "Rocket": plastic, electronic circuits, radar; sixth science: instrument board; rocket fuel,
      low density structures, control units; a rocket silo that builds a rocket from 30 parts.
    - Revival charge V (with control units) is needed from wave 15 on. Waves now go on until the end.
    - The finale: players in spacesuits standing by the silo when the rocket starts fly to the Moon. Air lasts
      5 minutes, each oxygen tank adds 2. The emitter, 250-350 tiles from the landing site, turns everyone
      within 100 tiles to stone for 8 seconds every 45 seconds. Dismantling it wins the game: no more waves,
      the crew comes home, and you can play on.
    - Defeat: a wave that finds the team still stone from the previous one ends the game.
  Changes:
    - The spacesuit protects in the cold and in the hot belt.
    - Launching a rocket no longer wins the game by itself.
    - Far continents have fewer big lakes near their coasts.

Version: 0.7.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Major Features:
    - Continents: the game starts on a temperate continent about 1600 tiles across. The cold north and the
      hot south lie beyond some 700 tiles of deep ocean. A shelf of shallow water, 45-80 tiles wide, runs
      along every coast. Setting "Continents" (on by default; off gives ordinary land with the belts).
    - Landfill now fills only shallow water (the shelf, lakes). Deep landfill (stone, steel, mortar) fills
      deep water: a road across the ocean is possible but costly.
  Changes:
    - Lakes are shallow water; far inland the big lakes shrink.
    - Tin ore never generates within 160 tiles of the spawn.

Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Major Features:
    - Climate belts by latitude: north of -1200 is cold (snow, few trees, tungsten ore, bears), south of +1200
      is hot (dunes, sulfur deposits, all the crude oil, boars). The width is a map setting.
    - In the cold, furnaces, assemblers, drills, labs and boilers stop unless a burning brazier (epoch 1) or
      a steam radiator (epoch 3) is near. In the heat they work half of the time unless a water cooler
      (epoch 3) is near.
    - Without a fur coat in the cold or a light cloak in the heat, players lose 2 health per second.
  Features:
    - The top line shows the belt you are in and whether you are dressed for it.
    - Frozen buildings show a snowflake; the building window explains what it needs.
  Changes:
    - Bear dens only in the cold belt; boar wallows only outside it.

Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Major Features:
    - Epoch 3 "Steam and current": iron from the bloomery, then the blast furnace (iron and steel); pipes,
      offshore pump, boiler and steam engine; copper wire, poles and lamps; electric drills, inserters,
      assemblers and the laboratory; fast belts. Third science: mechanism.
    - Electric revival chamber: built over the old one, makes charges I to III. Charge III (with copper
      electrodes) is needed from wave 7 on. This version continues to wave 10.
    - Domestication: throw a capture net at a lair whose animals are dead, away, or turned to stone by a
      wave, and it becomes your pig farm, kennel or bear pen. Farms turn feed or meat into meat, hides
      and bones; they can be moved but not crafted.
  Changes:
    - Chutes upgrade into each other and into fast belts.
    - Iron ore is guaranteed near the spawn.
  Bugfixes:
    - Refusing a second revival chamber no longer fails for the electric one.

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Major Features:
    - Wild animals: wolves, boars and bears live in lairs from 150 tiles out (bears past 500). They attack
      anyone who comes within 25 tiles. Wolves and bears raid buildings at night now and then, starting
      with the first petrification wave. Boars only defend.
    - A petrification wave turns all animals to stone for 10 minutes.
    - Hunting: sling and sling stones, bow and arrows, palisade, crossbow turret that shoots arrows.
    - Animals drop meat, hides and bones: roast meat heals, hides are tanned into leather (leather jacket,
      crossbow turret), bones are ground into bone meal that makes a garden bear more.
  Features:
    - Setting "Wild animals": peaceful, calm (no raids), normal, dangerous.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Major Features:
    - Epoch 1 "Stone": clay, shells, saltpeter, fruit and fiber; campfire, kiln, workbench, scholar's desk,
      digger, lever arm, wooden chute, garden plot, fermentation vat, alembic. Research with clay tablets.
    - Petrification waves: the first comes after 2 hours, then every 4 hours and a bit sooner each time.
      A wave turns every player into a statue; the factory keeps working.
    - Revival chamber: one per team, makes a revival charge in 10 minutes into a sealed tank. A charge in
      the chamber wakes the whole team at once; without one, statues thaw after 5–15 minutes or as soon
      as the chamber makes a charge.
  Features:
    - The vanilla tech tree, starting kit, crash site and biters are gone. Metal ores are visible but need
      later tools. Kilns, alembics and the chamber burn charcoal only.
    - Every epoch 1 resource and fruit trees are guaranteed near the spawn.
    - Settings: wave difficulty (relaxed / normal / hard / off), new players start as statues.
    - This version ends after wave 3.
    - Incompatible with Space Age and Space Exploration (both replace the same tech tree).
  Locale:
    - English and Russian.
